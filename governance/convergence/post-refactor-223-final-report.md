# LumiNet post-refactor-223 peer-convergence release

## Decision summary

Post-refactor-223 absorbs the 13 supplied donor archives at natural granularity while preserving one target-native authority per runtime responsibility. The wave promotes bounded scanner planning, strict REALITY evidence, exact host-proxy release, a loopback HTTP→SOCKS compatibility bridge, transactional remote provisioning, DNS delegation/reliability/integrity planners, sustained transport truth, Android lifecycle/Quick Settings truth, native async and raw FFI hardening, subscription TLS diagnostics, a materialized redacted node catalogue with local hide semantics and exact-owner runtime activation, and semantic operator aliases.

Whole donor runtimes that would duplicate transport, VPN/TUN, DNS tunnel, authentication, system-proxy, or native-core ownership are superseded/reference-only rather than copied. The unrestricted `warp-relay` forwarding pattern is explicitly rejected as a negative guardrail source.

Cross-plane mutations require exact ownership; observational planners remain read-only; credentials stay out of operator node views; recovery evidence is retained on uncertain destructive transitions.
